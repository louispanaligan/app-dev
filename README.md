## 🧾 Overview
<!-- Syntax: ## Heading 2 -->

**The Godfather** is a *1972 American crime film* directed by **Francis Ford Coppola**, based on the novel by **Mario Puzo**.  
It chronicles the rise and transformation of **Michael Corleone** from a reluctant outsider to the ruthless head of the powerful **Corleone family**.
<!-- Syntax: Bold (** **), Italic (* *), Line Break (two spaces + Enter) -->

---

## 🎭 Main Characters
<!-- Syntax: ## Heading 2, Ordered List (1., 2., etc.) -->

1. **Don Vito Corleone** — The respected patriarch of the Corleone family.  
2. **Michael Corleone** — Initially distant, later becomes the new Godfather.  
3. **Sonny Corleone** — The impulsive and passionate eldest son.  
4. **Tom Hagen** — The loyal consigliere and family lawyer.  
5. **Kay Adams** — Michael’s love interest, symbolizing his lost innocence.
